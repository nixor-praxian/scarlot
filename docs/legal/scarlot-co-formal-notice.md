**Subject:** Domain name scarlot.co. Formal notice to transfer or take down.

Dear Mr Brückner,

We collaborated for approximately one year on the design of a joint venture, distinct from your platform operated at choice.ch. I contributed the problem definition and the concepts that gave rise to the brand "Scarlot". You contributed your pre-existing software infrastructure. Our collaboration was the subject of no written agreement, gave rise to no remuneration, and covered a period overlapping the incorporation of Choice Technologies AG on 5 September 2023.

Pursuant to article 530 of the Swiss Code of Obligations (CO), a simple partnership ("société simple") is constituted by the contract through which two or more persons agree to combine their efforts or their resources with a view to achieving a common goal. No written form is required. The duration of our collaboration, the unpaid nature of my contributions, and the complementary character of our respective inputs into a joint entrepreneurial project unambiguously satisfy the constituent elements of such a partnership.

The domain name scarlot.co was registered during this period. Whether this domain was registered in your personal name or in that of Choice Technologies AG is, for the purposes of the claims set out below, immaterial: in either case, the domain was acquired in furtherance of the purpose of the simple partnership and accordingly falls within the rules set out at articles 531 paragraph 2, 544 and 548 to 550 CO.

You are presently operating, at this address, an active landing page comprising a form for the collection of email addresses. This unilateral commercial exploitation of an asset arising from joint work constitutes, cumulatively: (i) an act of unfair competition within the meaning of article 3 paragraph 1 letter d of the Federal Act against Unfair Competition (LCD), creating a likelihood of confusion with the distinctive signs and the work product arising from our collaboration; (ii) unjust enrichment within the meaning of articles 62 et seq. CO; and (iii) an operation that further raises serious questions under the revised Federal Act on Data Protection (revFADP), as regards the personal data collected without a clearly established lawful basis.

My informal request for restitution was met with a refusal devoid of any articulated legal basis.

By the present notice, I formally put you on notice to take, within **fourteen (14) calendar days** from communication of this letter, one of the following two actions, in the alternative:

1. transfer the domain name scarlot.co to my name and sign the Full and Final Release annexed hereto; or
2. failing that, release the domain name and render it inactive (cancellation of the registration and removal of all content hosted at this address), and sign the same Release.

I further invite you to confirm to me in writing, within the same period, the identity of the currently registered holder of the domain name.

In the absence of execution within the period set, I expressly reserve all means and remedies available in law, including in particular:

- the action for liquidation of the simple partnership and for damages (articles 548 to 550 CO);
- the action under the LCD before the competent courts;
- the Uniform Domain-Name Dispute-Resolution Policy (UDRP) procedure before the Arbitration and Mediation Center of the World Intellectual Property Organization;
- abuse complaints to the registrar, to the registry .CO Internet S.A.S., and to the content delivery network operator Cloudflare;
- a complaint to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner concerning the data collection performed on the site.

I would be grateful if you would return to me, within the period set, a signed and dated counterpart of the annexed Release. The path of an amicable settlement, of which the annexed Release is the framework, appears to me preferable, in the well-understood interest of each party.

**Whereas:**

**it has been agreed as follows:**

### Article 1. Object

The Second Party undertakes, within fourteen (14) days from the signature of the present Release, alternatively to:

a) transfer ownership of the domain name scarlot.co to the First Party, at his cost and in accordance with the customary technical procedures of the relevant registry; or
b) failing that, cancel the registration of the domain name and remove all content hosted at this address.

### Article 2. Mutual Release

Subject to the full execution of article 1, the Parties grant each other a full and final release, for all purposes and without reservation, of any and all claims of whatever nature, contractual or non-contractual, founded on the simple partnership, on unfair competition, on unjust enrichment, on intellectual property, or on any other ground, that may arise from their past collaboration or from the use of the name "Scarlot" and its derivatives.

### Article 3. Covenant Not to Sue

Each Party undertakes to bring no judicial, administrative or arbitral action against the other Party in respect of any matter arising from the collaboration or from the present Release, it being understood that this undertaking does not prevent the enforcement of the present Release in the event of non-performance.

### Article 4. Confidentiality

The Parties undertake to maintain the confidentiality of the present Release and of its surrounding circumstances, subject to legal obligations and to the requirements of enforcement.

### Article 5. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

The present Release is governed by Swiss law. Any dispute relating to its validity, its interpretation or its performance shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the Canton of Zürich.
