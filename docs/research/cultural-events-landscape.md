# Cultural Events Ecosystem for Sex Workers (TDS) — Landscape Research

Compiled for Scarlot, April 2026. Focused on cultural festivals, conferences, art shows, and community gatherings comparable to Festip Toulouse (Festipxte 2023) and the SNAP Festival in Brussels.

Source seeds:
- https://iaata.info/Festipxte-2023-Visibilite-des-talents-TDS-6006.html
- https://www.instagram.com/festip_toulouse/
- https://www.halles.be/fr/ag/1669-snap-festival

## 1. Executive Summary

- A genuine sex-worker-led cultural ecosystem exists across Europe and North America: it spans annual festivals (SNAP, Festip, Whorehouse Cinema, Fête du Slip, Fesses-tival), academic-activist congresses (ESWA, Hurenkongress, Whore Studies), commemorative dates (June 2 and December 17), and embedded programming inside larger queer/feminist institutions (Pride, ICA London, MEM Montréal).
- Most events are organized by a small, recognizable set of collectives: in francophone Europe these are Aspasie (Geneva), Fleur de Pavé (Lausanne), Cabiria (Lyon), Grisélidis Toulouse, STRASS (France), Festip (Toulouse), Médecins du Monde / Roses d'Acier (Paris), UTSOPI + Art Whore Connection (Brussels). All are potentially partner-grade for Scarlot.
- 2025 was a structural year: it marked the 50th anniversary of the Saint-Nizier occupation in Lyon, with city-backed commemorations and an international convergence of TDS movements; the discovered network is therefore unusually warm and visible right now.
- Switzerland is well-served at the support level (Aspasie GE, Fleur de Pavé VD, Xenia BE, ProCoRe national) but only Geneva has a true sex-positive cultural festival (Fesses-tival, 8 editions). There is no Swiss equivalent to SNAP or Whorehouse Cinema.
- Funding/partnership models vary: most rely on COCOF/Loterie Romande/Loro/municipal grants and can accept ethical sponsors; commercial/platform partners are usually accepted only with strict alignment to a flat-fee, non-commission, sex-worker-centered ethic. These collectives are wary of commercial actors, so Scarlot's flat-subscription model is a credible entry point.

## 6. Switzerland-Specific Section (for Scarlot)

Switzerland's TDS cultural ecosystem is structurally smaller than Belgium's, France's, or Germany's. Findings:

**Cultural festivals in Switzerland**
- **Fesses-tival, Geneva**, the only Swiss event with a clear sex-positive cultural festival format and a recurring 8-edition track record. While not exclusively TDS-led, it has hosted Aspasie's photo/text exhibition (2023). It would be Scarlot's natural cultural anchor in Geneva. Next edition: Sep 23-27 2026.
- **Aspasie's own programming**, irregular but consistent (40th anniversary boat, Spoutnik 2021, Fesses-tival 2023, 17 Dec yearly). Aspasie is the Geneva-side gatekeeper for any Scarlot involvement in TDS culture.
- **La Fête du Slip, Lausanne**, Swiss flagship sex-positive festival 2012-2025, but **canceled for 2026**. There is now a programming gap in Lausanne that an entrepreneurial actor could fill.
- **Hurentag (June 2) and IDEVASW (Dec 17)**, observed in Geneva by Aspasie and in Vaud by Fleur de Pavé, but without large festival format. Switzerland tends to issue joint communiqués through ProCoRe rather than running cultural festivals.

**Operating support orgs (potential Scarlot beta-recruitment partners)**
- **Aspasie** (Geneva), most culturally active, runs the Centre Grisélidis Réal documentation centre; the obvious first partner.
- **Fleur de Pavé** (Lausanne / Vaud), bus + drop-in; less culturally programmed but present at the Hurentag/Dec 17.
- **Xenia** (Bern), focused on counseling; 40th anniversary moment recently.
- **ProCoRe** (federal umbrella), strategically the right entry point for any Switzerland-wide framing; 27 member orgs.

**Strategic implications for Scarlot**
- The Swiss federation route (ProCoRe) gives access to all 27 member orgs and would be more efficient than canton-by-canton outreach.
- For cultural visibility, the Fesses-tival (Geneva) is the highest-credibility venue partner already in Scarlot's home market.
- The Lausanne Fête du Slip cancellation is both a void and a warning sign about cultural-festival economics in CH.

## 7. Gaps and Dark Spots (where more digging is needed)

- **Zurich and the Alemannic-Swiss scene**: no TDS-specific cultural festival surfaced. The Zurich red light scene around Langstrasse exists culturally but seems not to host a formal TDS arts festival. Worth a direct call to Frauenberatung Sexarbeit Zürich (FIZ) and Flora Dora.
- **German-speaking TDS cinema**: Berlin PFF has a sex-worker section but no dedicated festival like queer.red. Worth looking for German equivalents, possibly under Hydra or BesD partnerships.
- **Quebec / Montréal beyond Stella**: no recurring festival surfaced, only one-off events tied to Stella's anniversaries. POSSE Quebec, Projet L.U.N.E. (Lutte, unité, écoute), AQPSUD partner-orgs may host cultural events worth investigating.
- **OTRAS / AFEMTRAS in Spain**: organizations are very politically active but no recurring festival format surfaced. Possible that street demos and talks are the primary cultural form.
- **Nordic (Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland)**: not searched in depth; the Nordic model creates a different climate. Rose Alliance (Sweden) and PION (Norway) are likely candidates.
- **Ireland**: not searched, Sex Workers Alliance Ireland (SWAI) likely organizes Dec 17 events.
- **Latin America**: not searched. AMMAR (Argentina), RedTraSex are the major regional networks.
- **Africa / Asia-Pacific**: out of geographic scope for Scarlot but NSWP regional chapters host cultural events.
- **Online editions**: Whorehouse Cinema went hybrid; SNAP partly streamed; would be worth confirming online attendance metrics.
- **Online activist art**: outside scope of formal events but a vibrant ecosystem (e.g., zines, OnlyFans-collective exhibitions).
- **Whether Festip Toulouse is still running in 2025-2026**: only the 2023 edition is well-documented. Their @festip_toulouse Instagram is the live source, direct outreach recommended.

## 9. Key takeaways for Scarlot's partnership/recruitment strategy

- The single most aligned event for Scarlot's first cultural touchpoint is the Geneva **Fesses-tival** (already partnered with Aspasie), with **SNAP! Brussels** (UTSOPI + AWC) as the regional flagship.
- The most efficient way to access multiple Swiss orgs is via **ProCoRe**.
- The structural moments that maximize visibility are **June 2 (Hurentag / Saint-Nizier)** and **December 17 (IDEVASW)**, both observed by Aspasie, Fleur de Pavé, Cabiria, STRASS, UTSOPI, and could anchor Scarlot's annual presence.
- The cancellation of **Fête du Slip** (Lausanne) creates an opening, and signals fragility in cultural-festival economics that an aligned commercial actor might responsibly help fund (without compromising the flat-subscription firewall).
